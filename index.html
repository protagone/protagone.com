01 · About

Who is protagone?

A name without a face. The work matters more than who does it.

Television tells you a vision. The feeds sell you to advertisers. Keep scrolling, keep paying, keep quiet.

We want the opposite: people who think for themselves, and the tools to do it.

So we build AI that makes people more capable.

And we help big ideas from the open-source community reach the world.

The goal: AI strong enough to help ordinary people take on injustice.

Stop watching their vision. Tell your own.
