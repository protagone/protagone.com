Who is protagone?

Every story has a protagonist. This one stepped out of the frame.

protagone is a name, not a person. It stands with the people who are never in the spotlight: the ones who use the web without owning any of it, and who are told to log in, pay up or look away.

The platforms build walls. We make doors, small ones at first, in the open and together with the open-source community. Some of what we build uses AI, and it answers to the people who use it, not to whoever pays for their data.

The work is the face. That is enough.

What we hold to

People before platforms

Every tool starts from what a person needs, not from what a company wants to measure.

Open by default

The code is public and free to use, change and share. Nothing hides behind a login.

Nothing to track

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. What you do stays in your own browser.

Built together

We help open-source projects, and they help us. Small groups, sharing what works.
