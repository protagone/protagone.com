Who is protagone?

Every story needs a protagonist. Ours walked off the set, and left the script on the chair for you.

Television sold us a vision, and the feeds took over where it stopped. A whole industry now earns its money by keeping you busy, distracted and paying. Keep scrolling. Keep paying. Keep quiet.

protagone is a name without a face, and a crew without a head office. We build technology that hands power back to the people who use it: open code, nothing tracking you, and AI that works for you instead of on you.

Stop watching their vision. Tell your own.

What we stand for

You come first

A tool earns its place by making you more capable. If it only makes someone else richer, we don't build it.

Open code

All our code is public and free to use, change and share. Read it before you trust it.

Nothing to track

Our tools need no account and show no ads. This site has no analytics either.

Built in the open

Our code lives on GitHub. Issues, translations and pull requests are welcome.
