Who is protagone?

Every story has a protagonist. This one stepped out of the frame.

protagone is a name, not a single person. It stands with the people who are never in the spotlight: the ones who use the web without owning any of it, and who are told to log in, pay up or look away.

The platforms build walls. We make doors, small ones at first, in the open and together with the open-source community. Some of what we build uses AI, and it answers to the people who use it, not to whoever pays for their data.

The work is the face. That is enough.

What we hold to

Your needs first

Every tool starts from what a person needs. What a company would like to measure does not count.

Open by default

The code is public and free to use, change and share. Nothing hides behind a login.

Nothing to track

Our tools need no account and show no ads. This site has no analytics either.

Built in the open

Our code lives on GitHub. Issues, translations and pull requests are welcome.
